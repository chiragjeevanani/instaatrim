Touchpoint 1: Homepage Top Ad Carousel (High-CPM Hero Takeover)
Prime Position #1

Overview: Positioned immediately below the top navigation and search bar. This is the highest-value ad placement in the app, capturing 100% of homepage impressions. It auto-slides through 3–4 premium brand campaigns with an official "Sponsored Spotlight" badge and 1-click offer claiming.

Screen Location Homepage (Top screen, immediately above Category Grid)
Ad Format & Specs 16:9 card (480x172px), auto-carousel (4.8s timer), dots indicator, CTA button
Target Advertisers Tier-1 Cosmetic & Haircare Brands (L'Oréal Paris, Dyson Pro, O3+, Forest Essentials)
User Interaction Tap opens Campaign Offer Modal with 1-click coupon apply & direct salon booking link
Commercial Model Tenancy ₹20,000–₹35,000/week/slide OR CPM ₹400–₹550

Touchpoint 6: In-Flow Brand Kit Upsell (Booking Flow Customizer)
Most Lucrative In-Funnel Ad

Overview: Integrated directly into Step 3 of the new BookingFlowModal. When customers book a treatment like a Facial, Hair Spa, or Body Polish, the app displays suggested brand formulations (e.g. L'Oréal Revitalift vs. Pond's Age Miracle vs. Biotique Botanical). Brands pay for placement to ensure salon staff use their product kit.

Screen Location Booking Flow (Step 3: Product & Brand Kit Customisation)
Ad Format & Specs Interactive brand cards with benefit tags ("Bestseller", "100% Ayurvedic"), select toggle
Target Advertisers Skincare, haircare, and spa product manufacturers seeking brand loyalty
User Interaction Customer selects preferred brand kit; salon prepares treatment accordingly
Commercial Model Pay-Per-Selection (CPA) ₹40–₹80 per customer kit selection + brand listing fee

Touchpoint 7: Post-Booking Confirmation & Appointment Tracker Screen
Post-Purchase Cross-Sell

Overview: Displayed on the BookingConfirmationPage and live appointment status screen. Customers spend 15–45 minutes tracking their appointment or traveling to the salon. This screen monetizes downstream consumer intent via aftercare skincare retail, nearby dining/coffee vouchers, and ride-hailing discounts.

Screen Location Booking Confirmation Page + Live Booking Status Progress Card
Ad Format & Specs Native Aftercare Recommendation Banner, partner coupon chip, affiliate link
Target Advertisers D2C post-treatment skincare brands, ride-hailing (Uber/Ola), cafes near salon
User Interaction Tap orders aftercare serum or claims a ride discount to reach the salon
Commercial Model Affiliate Revenue Share 15%–20% on retail orders OR ₹15/click

Executive Takeaway for Client: Combining all 7 touchpoints creates a complete, non-intrusive monetization ecosystem. By monetizing front-of-funnel browsing (Touchpoints 1–3), high-intent search & discovery (Touchpoints 4–5), in-funnel customization (Touchpoint 6), and post-booking cross-sells (Touchpoint 7), InstaaTrim unlocks multiple diversified revenue streams while maintaining an ultra-luxurious, verified user experience.
